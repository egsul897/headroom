# P0 / P1 gaps ranked by customer impact

1. **Affiliate transactions UNSUPPORTED** — discovery only; no simulation/capacity path.
2. **Subsidiary designation UNSUPPORTED** — redesignation never applied; acquisitions cannot package-clear.
3. **Guarantees PARTIAL** — entity-scope conditions only; no guarantee-chain execution.
4. **Verified remaining not production-authoritative** — host trusted issuer blocked; caller flags ignored (correct) but customers cannot publish AVAILABLE.
5. **No domain EXECUTABLE package-wide** — honest; means product must not claim PERMITTED.

## P1 — incorrect or incomplete for real packages

6. Refinancing maturity / continuity not first-class.
7. No separate Permitted Acquisition engine.
8. Asset-sale proceeds application incomplete without authority refs.
9. Amendment IR auto-recompile absent on product path.
10. Unseen packages (Chewy, Conmed, Gibraltar, …) not live Simulate data.
11. Shared capacity only when express constraints / authority sharedCapacityId present.
12. Springing maintenance covenants need certificate-driven inputs beyond seed financials.

Customer impact = wrong permission > missing remaining > incomplete family > UX polish.  
Refusing invented capacity is preferred over synthetic EXECUTABLE.
