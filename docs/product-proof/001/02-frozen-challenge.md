# Product Proof 001 — Frozen Challenge Questions

**Freeze time:** 2026-10-10T10:21:00Z (before pipeline execution and before independent legal comparison)  
**Package:** `mtn-2026-tenth-ar-credit-agreement`  
**Hypothetical transaction date:** **2026-10-10**  
**Currency:** USD (instrument currency)

> Transaction amounts are hypothetical test inputs, not claims about Vail Resorts’ actual financing plans.

---

## Questions Headroom must answer

### A. Debt incurrence permissions
What debt can the issuer and relevant restricted subsidiaries incur under the operative debt covenant?

### B. Lien permissions
What lien permissions are available to secure the proposed debt?

### C. Shared baskets / anti-stacking
Which baskets share capacity or are subject to anti-stacking rules?

### D. Ratios, conditions, definitions
Which ratios, conditions, and definitions affect those permissions?

### E. Historical usage / outstanding debt
What historical usage or outstanding debt must be considered?

### F. Gross and remaining capacity
What is the verified gross capacity, and what is the verified remaining capacity, if determinable?

### G. $50 million secured debt
Can the issuer incur a proposed **$50,000,000** secured debt transaction on **2026-10-10**?

### H. $100 million secured debt
What changes if the proposed amount is **$100,000,000**?

### I. Subsequent capacity
What is the effect on subsequent available capacity after such a transaction?
