## B. Current §6.11 reconstruction

**Conclusive finding, not UNKNOWN_REVIEW_REQUIRED**: "Section 6.11(i)" was a **drafting cross-reference error present in the original agreement**, referenced by two live incurrence-condition provisions (§6.01(p) general Permitted Debt catch-all, and the unsecured/junior-lien prong of the Incremental Amount's Ratio-Based Incremental Facility), and **corrected by Amendment No. 4**.

- **Which amendment created it**: none — it was never a real clause. It first appears (as an error) in the original July 1, 2022 Credit Agreement.
- **Which amendment resolved it**: Amendment No. 4, September 26, 2025. The redlined text reads literally `Section 6.11( i a )` — i.e., "(i)" struck through and replaced with "(a)" — in the Incremental Amount definition's unsecured/junior-lien prong. (Source: Exhibit 10.1 to the 9/26/2025 8-K, Incremental Amount definition.)
- **Exact operative language today**: "...(ii) in the case of any Incremental Facility that is unsecured or that is secured by Liens on the Collateral on a junior basis..., the Total Net Leverage Ratio is not greater than the then-applicable ratio in the Financial Covenant set forth in Section 6.11(a)..."
- **What financial test it contains**: none directly — it's a cross-reference to §6.11(a), the Total Net Leverage Ratio maintenance covenant (see §C for its current terms).
- **Which debt-incurrence permissions incorporate it**: (1) CA §6.01(p), general Permitted Debt catch-all — "the Total Net Leverage Ratio on a Pro Forma Basis is not greater than the applicable ratio in the Financial Covenant set forth in Section 6.11(a)" (this specific occurrence of "(i)" in §6.01(p) was **not independently confirmed corrected** — the targeted search that found the "(i)→(a)" redline was in the Incremental Amount definition specifically; §6.01(p)'s own cross-reference was read from the original agreement and from Amendments 1–3's unchanged restated copies, but Amendment 4's restated §6.01(p) text was not independently re-extracted in this pass — see §H); (2) the Incremental Amount's Ratio-Based Incremental Facility, unsecured/junior prong (confirmed corrected).
- **Tested before or after giving effect to the proposed transaction**: after — "on a Pro Forma Basis after giving effect to the incurrence of such Indebtedness."
- **Acquisition-related, holiday, step-up, or other adjustment**: yes — see §C's Financial Covenant Step-Up/Cool-Down mechanic, which applies to §6.11(a) directly and therefore flows through to every provision (like the corrected Incremental Amount prong) that cross-references "the then-applicable ratio in... Section 6.11(a)."
- **Applicability different from the ordinary maintenance covenant**: no — once corrected to "(a)," it's the *same* ratio and the *same* currently-applicable level (including any active step-up) as the maintenance covenant itself, just applied as an *incurrence* condition rather than a quarterly maintenance test. This is a real, confirmed instance of Conclusion 5's distinction (maintenance covenant vs. incurrence condition) — same numeric threshold, two different roles.
